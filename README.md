About Me

I am a Digital Business and Data Science student based in Berlin/Potsdam, focused on machine learning, causal inference, time series forecasting, and practical data products.

I enjoy building systems that connect research ideas with useful workflows, including automated reporting, forecasting pipelines, causal estimation, and AI-assisted analysis.

Currently, I am working on:

Causal inference and treatment-effect estimation

Financial and energy time-series forecasting

Reproducible machine-learning pipelines

Automated analytical reporting

AI agents and reinforcement-learning ideas

Current Focus

Area

What I am building or studying

Causal Inference

IPW, AIPW, meta-learners, sensitivity analysis, and treatment-effect estimation

Time Series Forecasting

ARIMA, GARCH, LightGBM, Prophet, LSTM, N-BEATS, and benchmarking workflows

ML Engineering

Python pipelines, automated reports, model evaluation, and reproducible notebooks

AI Systems

LLM agents, reinforcement learning, and end-to-end analytical tools
